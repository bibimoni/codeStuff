\begin{problem}{Snow White}{standard input}{standard output}{0.25 seconds}{8 megabytes}

One day, Snow White and the seven dwarfs were taking a walk in the park.

At that time, there were $n$ men in the park. 
All $n$ of these men told Snow White their names and heights. 
Despite living with the seven dwarfs for a long time, Snow White had never asked for their names. 
Snow White only remembered that all seven dwarfs had the exact same height, and no one else had the same height as these dwarfs.

Since the number of men is very large, Snow White asks you to write a program that reads the names and heights of the $n$ men in the park and prints the names of the seven dwarfs.

\InputFile
The first line contains an integer $n$ ($7 \le n \le 100$) --- the number of men in the park.

In the next $n$ lines, the $i$-th line contains a string $S[i]$ and an integer $H[i]$. 
Here, $S[i]$ is the name of the $i$-th man, consisting of 1 to 10 English letters, and $H[i]$ ($1 \le H[i] \le 1000$) is his height.

\OutputFile
Print seven strings representing the names of the seven dwarfs, ordered according to their appearance in the input. 
The input guarantees that the seven dwarfs can be uniquely identified from the $n$ men according to the rules above.
