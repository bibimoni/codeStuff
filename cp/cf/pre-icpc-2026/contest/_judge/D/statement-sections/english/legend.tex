Two players play the following game. First, they agree on a positive integer $n$. Then they start with the value $p = 1$ and take turns, the first player moving first. On a turn, the current player multiplies $p$ by any integer $k$ such that $2 \le k \le 9$. The winner is the first player to make $p \ge n$.

Determine which player will win if both play optimally.
