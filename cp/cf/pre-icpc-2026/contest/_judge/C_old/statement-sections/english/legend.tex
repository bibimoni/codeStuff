Minh discovers a mysterious cave system consisting of many interconnected chambers.

The cave system forms a complete binary tree. The entrance of the cave is the root of the tree, and Minh initially stands at the entrance.

Each chamber may have a passage leading to its left child and a passage leading to its right child. Every chamber except the entrance also has a passage leading back to its parent.

A complete binary tree is a binary tree in which every level, except possibly the last, is completely filled, and all nodes on the last level are as far to the left as possible.

Minh does not know the structure of the cave or the total number of chambers.

Your task is to determine the total number of chambers in the cave by moving through it using the allowed queries.

For each test case, the number of chambers $N$ satisfies

$$1 \le N \le 10^{18}.$$
