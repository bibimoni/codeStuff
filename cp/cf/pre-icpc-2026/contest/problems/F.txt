# Problem F — Fairest of Them All
Time limit: 1s per test | Memory limit: 256 MB

## Statement
One day, Snow White and the seven dwarfs were taking a walk in the park.

At that time, there were $n$ men in the park. 
All $n$ of these men told Snow White their names and heights. 
Despite living with the seven dwarfs for a long time, Snow White had never asked for their names. 
Snow White only remembered that all seven dwarfs had the exact same height, and no one else had the same height as these dwarfs.

Since the number of men is very large, Snow White asks you to write a program that reads the names and heights of the $n$ men in the park and prints the names of the seven dwarfs.

## Input
The first line contains a single integer $n$ ($7 \le n \le 100$) --- the number of men in the park.

Each of the next $n$ lines contains a string $S$ and an integer $H$ ($1 \le |S| \le 10$, $1 \le H \le 1000$) --- the name of a man, consisting of English letters, and his height.

It is guaranteed that exactly seven of the men have the same height, and that no other man has that height, so the seven dwarfs can be uniquely identified.

## Output
Print seven strings --- the names of the seven dwarfs, ordered according to their appearance in the input.

## Example 1 — input
```
9
city 1
of 1
shining 1
stars 1
are 2
me 2
just 1
for 1
you 1
```
## Example 1 — output
```
city
of
shining
stars
just
for
you
```
