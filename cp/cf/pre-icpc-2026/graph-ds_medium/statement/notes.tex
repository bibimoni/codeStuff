In the example the atoll has $n = 3$ islands with causeway prices $a = (4, -2, 5)$ and flight prices $b = (3, 7, 1)$.

\begin{itemize}
\item \texttt{1 1 3}: paddling $1 \to 2 \to 3$ costs $4 - 2 = 2$, causeway $3$ directly costs $5$, and flying through the Nook costs $3 + 1 = 4$. The answer is $2$.
\item \texttt{2 2 6}: the rebate lagoon causeway is repriced, $a_2 := 6$.
\item \texttt{1 1 3}: now paddling $1 \to 2 \to 3$ costs $10$, so the Nook route ($3 + 1 = 4$) wins.
\item \texttt{3 1 -4}: flight to island $1$ goes on promo, $b_1 := -4$.
\item \texttt{1 1 3}: flying $1 \to \text{Nook} \to 3$ costs $-4 + 1 = -3$ --- the cooperative pays the traveler $3$ shells.
\item \texttt{1 1 4}: location $4$ is the Nook; the direct flight from island $1$ costs $-4$, and nothing beats a promo.
\end{itemize}
