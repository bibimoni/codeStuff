  > The Coral Loop is a ring of n tiny islands, numbered 1 to n around the atoll. Paddling causeway i, which links island i to island i+1 (causeway n loops back from island n to island 1), costs a[i] shells — though several causeways cross such gorgeous lagoons that the paddlers' cooperative pays you a rebate to use them, so some a[i] are negative.   > Lani runs the trip kiosk. By the Paddlers' Charter — twice-paddled water is wasted daylight — a route may land on each island at most once. Over the season she logs q events: a friend asks today's cheapest route from island s to island t (s < t); causeway x is repriced to w shells; or Nook flight x is repriced to w shells. After every question, Lani announces the best price.
  
  **Inline numeric example (n = 3):**
  
  Say a = (4, −2, 5) shells and b = (3, 7, 1).
